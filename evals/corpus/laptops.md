# Laptop Procurement and Refresh

## Standard devices
Engineers receive a laptop with 32 GB of memory. All other roles receive a laptop with 16 GB of memory.

## Refresh cycle
Laptops are replaced every three years. Earlier replacement is possible only when the IT team confirms a hardware fault.

## Leaving the company
Departing employees must return their laptop within five business days of their last day. Unreturned devices are remotely wiped and deducted from the final paycheck.
