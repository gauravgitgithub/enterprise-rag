# Parental Leave

## Entitlement
Birthing parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing parents, including adoptive parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave.

## Timing
Leave can start up to four weeks before the expected due date and must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Returning to work
Employees can return part-time at 60 percent of their hours for the first four weeks back without a change in pay.
