# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements need approval from a vice president.

## Equipment stipend
Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD for a desk, chair or monitor. The stipend is paid once per employee, not once per year.

## Working abroad
Working from another country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year because of tax and immigration rules. Longer stays require a review by the people operations team.
