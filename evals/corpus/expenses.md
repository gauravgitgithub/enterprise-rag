# Expense Reimbursement

## Deadlines
Submit expense reports within 60 days of the expense date. Reports submitted after 60 days are rejected automatically and cannot be appealed.

## Receipts
Every expense above 25 USD needs an itemised receipt. Card statements alone are not accepted as receipts.

## Payment
Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly payroll run. Reimbursements are paid in the employee's home currency at the exchange rate on the date of the expense.
