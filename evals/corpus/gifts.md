# Gifts and Hospitality

## Accepting gifts
Employees may accept gifts from vendors or customers worth up to 100 USD per year from any single source. Cash and gift cards are never acceptable, whatever their value.

## Hospitality
Business meals with vendors are allowed if they are reasonable and infrequent. Hospitality during an open procurement with that vendor must be declined.

## Declaring gifts
Gifts above the limit must be declared in the compliance portal within 10 days and are usually donated to charity.
