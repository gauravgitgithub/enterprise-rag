# On-Call Compensation

## Standby pay
Engineers on the on-call rotation receive 200 USD for each week they are on standby, whether or not they are paged.

## Incident time
Time spent responding to a page outside working hours is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate, rounded up to the nearest 30 minutes.

## Rest
After an overnight page that lasts more than two hours, the engineer may start work late the next day without using leave.
