# Support Team Weekend Coverage

## Weekend shifts
Customer support agents cover weekends on a rotating schedule. Each agent works at most two weekends per month.

## Weekend pay
Agents receive a flat 150 USD for each weekend shift they work. Shifts on public holidays are paid at 1.25 times the agent's normal rate.

## Escalations
Agents escalate production outages to the engineering on-call rotation instead of trying to fix them.
