# Customer Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for annual plans. Monthly plans are not refundable after the first 7 days. Refunds are never issued for usage-based overage charges.

## How to request
Support agents file refund requests in the billing console under "Adjustments". Requests above 5,000 USD need written approval from the finance controller before they are processed.

## Processing time
Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 10 business days. Card refunds can take one extra statement cycle to appear.
