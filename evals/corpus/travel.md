# Business Travel Policy

## Booking
All flights and hotels must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days before departure. Bookings made later need a manager's approval recorded in the portal.

## Class of travel
Economy class is the default. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours of continuous flying time.

## Daily allowance
The daily meal allowance is 75 USD in domestic cities and 95 USD abroad. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any allowance.
