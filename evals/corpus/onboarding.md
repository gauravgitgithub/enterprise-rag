# New Hire Onboarding

## First week
New hires complete security awareness training and sign the acceptable use policy during their first week. Access to production systems is granted only after both are done.

## Buddy program
Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for their first 90 days.

## Probation
The probation period lasts six months. Managers hold a formal check-in at the end of month three.
