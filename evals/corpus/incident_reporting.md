# Security Incident Reporting

## What to report
Report lost or stolen laptops, phishing emails you clicked, suspected malware and any accidental sharing of customer data.

## How fast
Report every suspected incident to the security team within one hour of noticing it, using the #sec-incidents channel or the security hotline. Do not wait until you are sure.

## After reporting
Do not try to investigate or clean up a compromised machine yourself. Disconnect it from the network and leave it powered on so evidence is preserved.
